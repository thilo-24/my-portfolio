## Project Overview

This project focused on creating a clean, high-impact brand identity system for a modern music and electronics retail store. The goal was to produce a versatile logo mark that works seamlessly across print collateral, digital templates, and physical storefront signage.

### Key Deliverables

* **Primary Logo & Icon Mark:** Scalable vector logo optimized for dark and light backgrounds.
* **Social Media Templates:** High-engagement layout assets for seasonal promotions.
* **Store Signage:** High-resolution vector files ready for large-format physical print.

### Design Process

1. **Concept & Sketching:** Exploring typography treatments blending musical and electronic motifs.
2. **Vectorization:** Refining geometries in Adobe Illustrator for exact proportions.
3. **Mockups & Guidelines:** Creating standardized asset packages for multi-channel usage.
